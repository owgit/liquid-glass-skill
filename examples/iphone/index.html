How it forms

Charged particles follow Earth’s magnetic field toward the poles. When they collide with gas in the upper atmosphere, the gas emits light. Oxygen can glow green or red, while nitrogen can add blue and purple.

Viewing tips

Find a clear stretch of dark sky away from streetlights. Give your eyes time to adjust, then watch for faint arcs that brighten or shift into curtains.
