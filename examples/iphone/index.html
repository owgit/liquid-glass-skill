Tab bar

The bar at the bottom of a mobile page, with a round button beside it. Every tab is a link with a one-word label and a 44-pixel target, and only the selected tab takes the accent colour. With the glass on, a lens marks the selected tab, glides to the one you tap, and grows and follows when you hold and drag.

.tabs {
  --glass-depth: 1.6;
  --glass-dome: 1.4;
  --glass-frost: .3;
  --glass-tint: rgba(6, 24, 20, .4);
  --glass-lens: 100 0 30;
}

A lens you can move

Drag it across the tiles, or focus it and use the arrow keys. A thicker, more domed pane magnifies what is behind it. The lens is shown only while the glass effect is on.
